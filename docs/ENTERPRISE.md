# Enterprise Notes

AXZ-CSL is designed for open-source adoption first and enterprise extension second.

## Recommended public positioning

- The GitHub repository is the open proof kernel.
- Paid work is offered as integrations, certificates, custom rule packs, private deployments, and support.
- The mathematical claim remains bounded finite-trace replay soundness.

## Investor-safe sentence

AXZ-CSL separates expensive search from lightweight receipt checking: external systems generate finite mutation traces, and AXZ-CSL verifies those traces against declared local laws with machine-checkable proof discipline.

## What not to claim

Do not claim P=NP, RH, universal solver correctness, or global bug-free software.
